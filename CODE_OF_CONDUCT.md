# Code of Conduct

Skynode follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and specific in issues and reviews, assume good faith, and keep discussion about the work, not the people. Harassment, personal attacks, and posting private information about someone are not allowed.

Report a concern through GitHub's private reporting (the repository's **Security** tab, **Report a vulnerability**) or by opening a discussion for non-sensitive matters. Reports are read by the maintainer and handled privately.
