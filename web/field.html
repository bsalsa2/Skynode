One-time setup needed

Sign-in needs a Google client ID. Follow the steps in web/README.md under “Phone upload page”, then paste the ID into web/src/field-config.js.

    This page asks Google for one narrow permission: to create and write files it makes itself. It cannot read the rest of your Drive. Your clips go straight from your phone to your Drive; nothing passes through Skynode’s servers. Loading Google’s sign-in tells Google you opened this page.
